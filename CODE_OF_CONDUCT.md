# Code of conduct

KeyJutsu follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, criticise ideas rather than
people, and make it easy for newcomers to take part. Harassment, personal
attacks and discriminatory language are not acceptable in any project space:
issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere the project is represented.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainers through GitHub's private
reporting on this repository. Reports are handled in confidence, and the
people involved in a report take no part in deciding its outcome.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that break this code,
and may warn, suspend or ban contributors, following the Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.
